Station 2141 uses optional, privacy-focused analytics for one purpose: to understand which posts readers choose and to publish simple visitor statistics. It is not used for advertising, cross-site tracking, personal profiles, or identifying individual readers.

## What is counted

If you allow counting, the site sends the current page path and title to [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/). Station 2141 is configured to retain aggregate counts by page and time period, not individual browsing histories.

To avoid counting an immediate reload as another visit, GoatCounter briefly derives a session from the site, IP address, and browser user-agent. The IP address and full user-agent are not written to disk; the temporary association is kept in memory for up to eight hours and then discarded. The analytics tracker itself uses no cookies, local storage, or persistent visitor identifier.

## How the numbers are used

The author uses aggregate counts to see which posts attract readers. Overall and page-level counts are public. They are not sold or shared for advertising.

The figures shown as “today,” “this month,” and “this year” are anonymous visits, not a precise count of distinct human beings. A returning reader may be counted again after the temporary session expires, and blockers or network conditions may prevent a visit from being counted.

## Your choice

No visit-recording request is sent until you select **Allow counting**. The visitor widget may still fetch already-aggregated public totals from GoatCounter; that read-only request does not add your visit to the statistics and sends no page path or referrer. If you choose **Do not count me**, your page visit is not sent to GoatCounter. A first-party consent cookie stores only that choice so the site can remember it; it contains no visitor identifier. You can change the choice at any time through **Change counting choice** in the footer or visitor widget.

GoatCounter's current data-handling details are available in its [privacy documentation](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).
